## 2025-02-25 - Path.resolve() vs os.path.abspath() latency
**Learning:** `Path.resolve()` interacts with the filesystem at every level to resolve symlinks and check existence, adding massive latency overhead in performance-critical loops (like generating SQLite cache keys). `os.path.abspath()` is fundamentally string-based and significantly faster, though it sacrifices symlink deduplication.
**Action:** When generating string absolute paths from `pathlib.Path` objects in performance-critical loops or batch operations where strict symlink resolution isn't actively required, prefer `os.path.abspath(p)` over `str(p.resolve())`.
